Case Study · 02 of 09

We deleted hundreds of thousands of fake followers — then grew 250,000 real ones.

Diana came to us with thousands of inflated followers, no brand identity, and a fear of the camera. We solved the human first. The metrics followed.

Client Diana Clinics
Field Medical · Aesthetics & Wellness
Scope Full Brand Build · Content Strategy · Growth · Camera Coaching
Timeline Multi-phase rebuild
Starting point
Inflated follower count · No identity · Camera-shy founder
→
Result
250,000+ engaged real followers · Self-sustaining content engine · High conversion
01 · The Challenge

When Diana came to us, the numbers looked fine on paper.

The accounts were loaded with fake, purchased followers. A legacy decision that had quietly sabotaged every piece of content she had ever posted. The algorithm could not push her work because the audience signals were fraudulent. She was shouting into a room full of empty seats.

On top of that: no brand identity. No visual language. And the deepest barrier — camera shyness from an otherwise exceptional doctor.

When Diana came to us, the numbers looked fine on paper. Thousands of followers. Accounts active for years. But engagement was dead — because no real people were ever there to begin with.

02 · Our Strategy

What we built — and why.

Three pillars carried the work. None of them are template moves. All of them were earned the hard way.

01

Counterintuitive cleanup

We deleted the fake followers first. Painful on paper. Liberating in practice. The algorithm started reading clean signals from real humans.

02

Confidence before content

Camera shyness is not a technical problem. It is a confidence problem. We coached Diana in her own clinic environment until the lens disappeared.

03

Identity from scratch

A full visual identity, tone of voice, and content architecture. The brand became recognisable in the first three frames of any video.

03 · The Receipts

Numbers earned. Receipts kept.

250K Real engaged followers
0 Fake followers remaining
∞ Self-sustaining engine
Authenticity scales. Inflated audiences are a tax. Deleting fake followers is the fastest way to grow a real brand.
— The Optiiimum team
