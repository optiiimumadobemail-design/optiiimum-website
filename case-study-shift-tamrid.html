Case Study · 03 of 09

A profession of millions had zero platforms. We built it from scratch — and a single video crossed 1M views.

Nursing is the backbone of every healthcare system on earth. In Arabic medical media, it was nearly invisible. Shift Tamrid changed that.

Client Shift Tamrid
Field Medical · Nursing · Podcast · Professional Media
Scope Brand Build · Social Launch · Content Strategy · Podcast Production · Ambassador Program
Timeline Build from zero
Starting point
0 followers · 0 content · 0 platform
→
Result
95,000+ authentic followers · 1M+ views on a single video · First Arab nursing podcast
01 · The Challenge

Nursing is indispensable.

Shift Tamrid came to us with a vision and nothing else: no followers, no content, no social media. Just a conviction that nurses deserved a platform — and that the right platform, built correctly, could become the defining voice of an entire profession.

The challenge was not building from zero. It was building something that had never existed in this region.

Nursing is indispensable. In Arabic medical media, it was treated as background. There was no podcast that spoke to the nursing world. No media brand that treated it with the same intellectual seriousness afforded to doctors and specialists.

02 · Our Strategy

What we built — and why.

Three pillars carried the work. None of them are template moves. All of them were earned the hard way.

01

Podcast as flagship

A long-form podcast featuring the leading voices in Arab medicine and nursing. The episodes became the credibility engine.

02

Short-form virality

Hooks built for the algorithm, dignity built for the profession. A single video crossed 1,000,000 views.

03

Ambassador program

Trained nurses themselves became the brand. The platform stopped being a media outlet and started being a community.

03 · The Receipts

Numbers earned. Receipts kept.

95K+ Authentic followers
1M+ Single-video views
1st Arab nursing podcast
Categories without a media voice are not gaps in the market. They are entire markets.
